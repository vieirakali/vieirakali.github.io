At Hurb, I helped scale and modernize the design system to unify UI components across squads. I improved the system’s consistency, maintainability, and onboarding documentation to accelerate product development.

- Advanced the Design System by building and refining components, aligning with Design and Engineering, and documenting guidelines.
- Optimized Figma libraries by reorganizing architecture, leveraging new features, and consolidating assets to drive adoption.
- Defined visual identity standards (colors, icons, typography) and ensured consistency across teams.
- Bridged Design and Engineering through my front-end expertise; led knowledge-sharing sessions on CSS, HTML, and JS frameworks.

## Problem

Teams were duplicating UI elements, leading to design inconsistencies and inefficiencies in delivery. There was no clear component governance or scalable token system.

## Process

- Ran audits of current usage across squads.
- Interviewed designers and engineers to identify friction points.
- Created a single source-of-truth Figma library and contribution process.

## Design decisions

- Organized components using atomic principles.
- Built variants and tokenized color, spacing, and typography.
- Created usage guidelines directly in Figma for faster onboarding.

## Outcome

Adoption of the Design System increased. Squad designers reduced duplication by half, and developers had a cleaner handoff pipeline through component references.
