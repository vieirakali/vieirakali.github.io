As a Design Technologist at Dell, I prototyped and delivered highly interactive experiences using AEM and Vue.js. I focused on UI design, micro-interactions, and scalable component handoffs.

- Optimized user engagement through animation, AEM integration, and scalable UI components.
- Built on Dell Design System guidelines, adding new components like dynamic cards, modals, and filters.
- Front-end in Vue.js integrated with Adobe Experience Manager, with all interactions in CSS.

## Problem

Product pages lacked motion and dynamic interaction. Designers struggled to communicate experience goals to engineers, resulting in static or misaligned builds.

## Process

- Worked directly with UX to prototype transitions.
- Created component templates in Vue.js.
- Delivered interaction specs through functional prototypes instead of static mockups.

## Design decisions

- Implemented responsive logic to adapt animations to device capabilities.
- Followed motion accessibility guidelines to support reduced-motion users.

## Outcome

My components became reusable templates across marketing campaigns, and stakeholder feedback highlighted a smoother UX and higher engagement. Development cycle time dropped with clear interaction specs.
